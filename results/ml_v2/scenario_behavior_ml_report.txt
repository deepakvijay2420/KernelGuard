KernelGuard Scenario-Level Behavioral ML Evaluation
===================================================

Purpose:
Evaluate whether behavioral and temporal telemetry can distinguish benign and attack scenarios while preventing repetitions of the same scenario from appearing in both training and testing.

Excluded information:
- risk
- reason
- action
- task_id
- attack_id
- label
- exact executable identity

Evaluation:
Scenario-level GroupShuffleSplit was used.
All repetitions belonging to the same scenario were kept in the same partition.

Important limitation:
This is a controlled experimental dataset. The result demonstrates performance on the generated scenarios and does not establish generalization to all real-world Agentic AI attacks.
